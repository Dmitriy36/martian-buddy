Privacy Policy

Night Desk Trivia · Effective 10/11/2026

This policy explains what information Night Desk Trivia ("the app") uses and how. The app is a hobby project that runs a trivia game in the live chat of one YouTube live stream: Yours Truly, Johnny Dollar 24/7.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By interacting with the app, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service , and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy .

Information the app uses

The app reads messages posted in the stream's public live chat, including each message's text and the poster's public YouTube channel name. It reads chat only for a short window after each trivia question, and only to check whether a message is a correct answer.

The app does not collect or receive your email address, contacts, private messages, payment information, or any data from outside the stream's public live chat.

How the information is used

Chat messages are used only to find the first correct answer to the current question and to post the winner's name in chat so they can receive their prize.

Storage and sharing

The app does not store chat messages after a question ends. It may keep basic technical logs, such as the time a question was posted and the name of its winner, to keep the game running correctly. This information is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Your choices

Participation is optional. If you do not want your chat messages checked by the app, you can simply not answer trivia questions. The stream owner's authorization for the app can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page .

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
