Level 1: Mars City

Overview

This is the introductory level where you arrive at Mars City and get familiar with the environment before things go wrong.

Walkthrough

Follow the security guard through the facility. Pick up your PDA from reception. Explore the area and talk to NPCs to get a feel for the story.

Locker Codes

  • Locker #001: Code 123 - Contains armor shards
  • Locker #034: Code 531 - Contains ammo and health
Pro Tip: Make sure to grab all the armor and ammo you can before the invasion starts!
